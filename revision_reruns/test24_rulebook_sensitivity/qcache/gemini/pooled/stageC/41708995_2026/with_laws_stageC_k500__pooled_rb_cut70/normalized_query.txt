Background: To synthesize and critically appraise the evidence on early-phase fluid biomarkers in acute ischemic stroke, stratify findings by clinically relevant therapeutic time windows, and identify the most promising protein, metabolite, and nucleic/ribonucleic-acid markers for rapid AIS diagnosis and stroke-mimic differentiation. This pseudo abstract is optimized for retrieval of likely in-scope biomedical studies.
Methods: We conducted a structured evidence study aligned to the review question, with eligibility criteria defined as follows: Systematic reviews and meta-analyses including studies on fluid biomarkers used to differentiate ischemic stroke from stroke mimics or controls; fluid sampling <72 h; confirmatory MRI with diffusion sequences as the standard of truth; human subjects; articles in English, German, Dutch, Italian, French, Spanish, or Portuguese. Out-of-scope studies were excluded using these constraints: Studies comparing ischemic stroke versus hemorrhagic stroke or controls; systematic reviews not reporting biomarker-level differences between AIS and stroke mimics; systematic reviews or meta-analyses without primary-study data; primary studies; narrative reviews; conference abstracts.
Results: Candidate studies meeting these criteria showed consistent directional effects across key outcomes relevant to the review question, and reported findings in a clinically interpretable way without relying on unsupported exact statistics.
Conclusions: The findings support relevance to the review objective and justify prioritization for downstream screening under the predefined inclusion criteria.
