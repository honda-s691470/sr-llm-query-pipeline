Background: To compile and analyse all available RA referral criteria since January 2001 until October 2023. This pseudo abstract is optimized for retrieval of likely in-scope biomedical studies.
Methods: We conducted a structured evidence study aligned to the review question, with eligibility criteria defined as follows: Studies were only included in this review if they provided Rheumatoid Arthritis referral criteria or Inflammatory / Early Arthritis criteria directed at RA diagnosis. There was no language restriction. Out-of-scope studies were excluded using these constraints: Studies with generic referral criteria to Rheumatology or a full range of diseases without specific RA-directed criteria were not included, as they are not disease specific. Studies involving only patient self-report questionnaires were not included as they are more directed towards patient than clinician assessment. In addition, studies concerning screening for the general population with/without observation of patients by Rheumatologists, before any GP observation, were also not included as they are not in the scope of this review.
Results: Candidate studies meeting these criteria showed consistent directional effects across key outcomes relevant to the review question, and reported findings in a clinically interpretable way without relying on unsupported exact statistics.
Conclusions: The findings support relevance to the review objective and justify prioritization for downstream screening under the predefined inclusion criteria.
