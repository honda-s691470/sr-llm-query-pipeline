## Scope

This run covers the Run3 rulebook × Claude provider × all 24 held-out test
papers leg of the sensitivity matrix in
`handoff_test24_run2_run3_pooled_rulebook_metrics.md`, Stage 2 cutoff = cut
bottom 70% (retain ~top 30%, gate=1000/floor=1000). Outputs use a distinct
`with_laws_run3_rb_test_eval` condition and did not touch or overwrite any
Run2 outputs (`with_laws_run2_rb_test_eval`, `with_laws_run2_rb_test_eval_p4manual`)
or their PubMed metadata caches.

Pooled-consensus and the Codex/Gemini legs are not included here.

## Method notes

Identical method to the Run2 analysis: queries generated by 5 in-process
Claude Sonnet 5 subagents (one per domain), following `test_prompt_with.md`
Steps 0–5 against the Run3 rulebook only, independently per paper, with no
gold PMID/recall access. Scored via `score_existing_queries.py` (no provider
CLI). Stage 2 via `run_pipeline_prime_A_B_C.py --mode test` (no LLM calls;
rule-based NOT filters, BM25+MedCPT dense reranking, local-structured HyDE).

## Stage A' gate: 3 papers initially failed

`41470797_2025` (RA, raw hits 113,595), `41715180_2026` (CVD, raw hits
78,163), `41770755_2026` (Oncology, raw hits 22,737). Automated NOT-block
iteration reduced these to 34,514 / 39,464 / 12,188 respectively — still
over the 9,999 target.

`41715180_2026` and `41770755_2026` are the **same two papers** that
gate-failed under the Run2 rulebook — suggesting these two reviews are
structurally hard to narrow regardless of rulebook version (very common
disease + broad methodology/intervention terms).

### Tier 1 / Tier 1.5 attempt (safe, no recall lookup)

- `41470797_2025`: one Tier 1 candidate (`"Arthritis, Rheumatoid"[Mesh]`,
  backed by `"rheumatoid arthritis"[ti]`), but hits barely moved
  (34,514 → 34,327) — negligible.
- `41715180_2026`: no valid candidates (same conclusion as Run2 — both
  MeSH terms in the methodology block lack genuine same-concept tiab
  backups; the disease-block MeSH removal would itself be a disguised
  Tier 2 cut).
- `41770755_2026`: no valid candidates (same conclusion as Run2 — the
  bottleneck term `"Preoperative Care"[Mesh]` has no genuine tiab backup
  and is explicitly documented as unsafe from the Run2 finding).

Per the mandatory-iteration stop rule, all 3 should remain gate-failed
(Stage 1 only) under the normal Tier 1/1.5-only workflow.

### Tier 2 exception (user-authorized again, one-time, test-only, per paper)

The user again authorized a one-time Tier 2 test for these 3 papers.
Bottleneck terms were isolated by per-term standalone hit contribution,
then the broadest/most generic contributors were removed:

- **`41470797_2025`**: removed `"Oxidative Stress"[Mesh]` (29,041 of
  34,327 standalone), `"Inflammation"[Mesh]` + `inflammation[ti]`
  (6,070 / 2,651), and `"Antioxidants"[Mesh]` (8,811 of the
  intervention-side remainder) — 4 removals needed to clear gate.
  Final: 2,285 hits.
- **`41715180_2026`**: removed `"Prognosis"[Mesh]` (32,993 standalone) and
  `"risk stratification"[tiab]` (3,615) — same bottleneck term
  (`"Prognosis"[Mesh]`) as identified in the Run2 sensitivity analysis for
  this paper. Final: 8,761 hits.
- **`41770755_2026`**: removed `"Preoperative Care"[Mesh]` — identical to
  the Run2 finding for this paper. Final: 426 hits.

**Result — recall damage, consistent with Run2 and with why Tier 2 is
forbidden:**

`41770755_2026`'s Tier 2 result is nearly identical to Run2's (same term

inclusion criteria (prehabilitation has no strong MeSH anchor other than
